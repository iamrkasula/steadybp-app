Adding a reading

Take a reading with your cuff, tap Add a reading and type the top number, the bottom number and, if you like, your pulse. The keypad moves on by itself. Taking a second reading? Add it within 10 minutes and both count as one session.

How the ranges work

SteadyBP uses the American Heart Association's blood pressure categories for adults; a reading takes the higher category of its two numbers. SteadyBP doesn't measure blood pressure and isn't medical advice. Talk to your doctor about your readings. If a reading is very high and you have symptoms such as chest pain, shortness of breath or trouble speaking, call 911.

Reports for your doctor

Open the Report tab, choose the dates and tap Share PDF, Save to Files or Print. Export all readings makes a CSV file for a spreadsheet.

iCloud

With iCloud sync on, readings stay the same on your iPhones signed in to the same Apple Account, and readings typed on your Apple Watch reach your iPhone. If Settings → iCloud sync says iCloud is off, open the Settings app, tap your name and sign in to iCloud.

Readings for someone else

Settings → People → Add a person. A switcher then appears on Overview, History and Report, and each person gets their own report. Apple Watch entry is for your own readings.

Reminders don't appear

Open the Settings app → Notifications → SteadyBP and turn on Allow Notifications.

Still stuck, or an idea?

Request features and vote on the feature request board, or report a problem at github.com/iamrkasula/steadybp-app/issues. Both are public, so please don't include personal or health details.
