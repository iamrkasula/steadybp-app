Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

SteadyBP is a blood pressure log. You type in the numbers from your own cuff; SteadyBP stores them, shows their range and makes reports. Your readings never reach us. The only thing that ever comes to us is a crash report, described below.

Your readings

  • On your iPhone and Apple Watch. Readings, people you add, medications, notes and tags are stored on your devices.
  • In your own iCloud. With iCloud sync on (the default), they are kept in your private iCloud database so they stay the same on your devices. Apple stores this data in your iCloud account; SteadyBP has no account and can't see it. You can turn sync off in Settings → iCloud sync.
  • Reports and exports are created on your iPhone. They go only where you send them, for example by email or AirDrop.

Reminders

Reading and medication reminders are notifications set up and shown by your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server.

Crash reports

If the App Store or TestFlight version of SteadyBP crashes or stops responding, it sends a crash report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) so the problem can be found and fixed. A report contains the technical details of the crash: the app version, the code that was running, the device model, iOS version, free memory and similar device state, and a random install identifier.

A report does not contain your readings, names, medications, notes, screenshots or a recording of the screen. IP addresses are not stored (IP scrubbing is on). Reports are not linked to you, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else. About one in ten sessions also sends performance timings for the same purpose.

What SteadyBP does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No server of our own: your readings are never sent to us.
  • No Apple Health access.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes every reading, person and medication on this iPhone and, with iCloud sync on, in your iCloud on all your devices. Deleting the app removes what is stored on that device.

Children

SteadyBP collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/iamrkasula/steadybp-app/issues. Issues there are public, so please don't include personal or health details.
