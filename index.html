Log in seconds

A big keypad moves from the top number to the bottom number to pulse by itself.

See the range

Every reading shows its American Heart Association category in words, never colour alone.

Mornings and evenings

Seven-day averages for each, with double readings counted once, and a 30-day chart.

A report your doctor can use

A PDF with averages, counts, a chart and every reading. CSV export of everything, any time.

For the people you care for

Keep readings and medications for up to six people, each with their own report.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Readings stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

SteadyBP doesn't measure blood pressure and isn't medical advice. Talk to your doctor about your readings.
